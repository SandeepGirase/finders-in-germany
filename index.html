3 — Why this exists

Germany is generous to students. Its paperwork isn't generous with your time.

Public universities charge little to no tuition, and the country actively wants international graduates to stay and work. But the process to get there runs through a dozen offices — the Auswärtiges Amt, the Ausländerbehörde, the Bürgeramt, the Finanzamt — each with its own forms and its own patience for mistakes.

We built Finders In Germany to translate that process into a plain, ordered checklist, written for someone doing this for the first time, from outside the country.

"Nobody tells you that registering your address comes before your bank account, and your bank account comes before your blocked account release. We wish someone had drawn us the map."
