06 — About

A guide written for the first-timer

Finders In Germany exists because the process of moving from application to Anmeldung is scattered across dozens of official websites, forums and outdated blog posts. We put the whole path in one place, in the order you'll actually need it.

What we do

We break the journey into five stages — universities, pre-arrival, accommodation, documentation, and part-time work — and explain each one in plain language, with the order of operations made explicit. Where a rule changes often (fees, thresholds, exact document lists), we say so and point you to confirm it directly with the relevant office.

What we're not

We are not a law firm, a visa agency, or affiliated with any German university, embassy or government office. Nothing on this site is legal or immigration advice — always confirm requirements with the Ausländerbehörde, your embassy, or your university's International Office before making decisions with financial or legal consequences.

Who it's for

International students who are:

  • researching German universities for the first time
  • preparing a student visa application
  • about to move, or recently arrived, in Germany
  • looking for a part-time job alongside their studies

Have a correction or a question?

We update this guide as rules change — tell us if something looks out of date.
