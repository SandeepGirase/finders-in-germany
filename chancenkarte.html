05 — Chancenkarte

The Opportunity Card, and how to become a student on it

The Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card) lets skilled non-EU professionals move to Germany to search for work without a job offer in hand. This page covers two separate audiences: applying for the Chancenkarte from your home country, and — if you're already in Germany on one — switching your residence permit over to study instead of job-search.

Who this page is for

If you're planning your move from abroad and think the Chancenkarte might suit you better than a direct student visa, read Part 1. If you're already living in Germany on a Chancenkarte and have decided to study instead of job-hunt, skip to Part 2.

Part 1

Getting a Chancenkarte from your home country

Introduced in June 2024 under §20a of the Residence Act (Aufenthaltsgesetz), the Chancenkarte is a job-search residence permit — issued for up to 12 months, with the right to work part-time (up to 20 hours a week) and take short trial jobs while you look for a role that matches your qualification.

Two ways to qualify — you only need one

RouteHow it works
Recognised qualification routeYour foreign university degree or a vocational qualification (minimum two years, state-recognised in the country you earned it) is fully recognised in Germany. Check your degree on the Anabin database — if it's rated "gleichwertig" or "entspricht," you qualify automatically, no points needed.
Points system routeIf your qualification isn't fully recognised, you can still qualify by scoring at least 6 points across qualification, work experience, language skills, age, and prior stays in Germany.

How the points are scored

CriteriaPoints available
Qualification (degree or vocational training)Up to 4 points
Recent, relevant work experience2–3 points
German or English language level1–3 points (German A2 and above scores; English needs to be B2 or higher to count)
Age2 points if under 35, 1 point if 35–40
Previous stay in Germany (6+ months in the last 5 years)1 point

The official Chancenkarte self-check calculator is the most reliable way to score yourself — points weighting and thresholds can be adjusted, so confirm your current total there rather than relying on this table alone.

Application steps

Applying from your home country

First

Check your qualification on Anabin

Search the Anabin database (or request a statement from the ZAB, the Central Office for Foreign Education) to see whether your degree is fully recognised. This determines which of the two routes above applies to you, and shapes every document you'll gather afterwards.

If using the points route

Run the official points self-check

Use the Chancenkarte points calculator to confirm you clear the 6-point minimum before you invest in translations, embassy fees, and appointment slots.

Ongoing

Prove your funds and health insurance

You need to show you can support yourself for the job-search period — roughly €1,091 per month (about €13,000 for a full year, as of 2026), via a blocked account, formal sponsorship letter, or a part-time job contract already lined up — plus health insurance valid for the stay.

Weeks to months

Apply at your local German embassy or consulate

Book your visa appointment as early as possible — processing commonly takes a few months. Bring your recognition result (or points evidence), language certificates, proof of funds, health insurance, and passport photos.

Once approved

Travel and complete your Anmeldung

On arrival, register your address the same way any resident does (see Documentation) — your Chancenkarte's clock is already running, so don't delay this step.

Part 2

Converting a Chancenkarte into a student residence permit

If you're already in Germany on a Chancenkarte and decide to study instead of continuing your job search, German law allows an in-country change of purpose (Zweckwechsel) — in most cases you do not need to leave Germany and reapply from your home country.

Prerequisite

Secure admission to a German university

You need a genuine admission letter (Zulassungsbescheid) before the Ausländerbehörde will consider the switch — a Chancenkarte alone does not qualify you to enrol, and an application in progress is usually not enough on its own.

Before you apply

Arrange student health insurance and funds

Swap or top up your existing proof of funds to meet the student-visa threshold (a blocked account is the most common route), and switch to public health insurance if your university requires it for enrolment — the same requirements covered in Pre-Arrival and Documentation apply here.

The application

Apply to your local Ausländerbehörde for a change of purpose

Submit your admission letter, proof of funds, health insurance certificate, passport and current Chancenkarte to request a residence permit for study purposes under §16b of the Residence Act. Book this appointment as soon as you have your admission letter — processing times vary by city and office.

Timing

Apply well before your Chancenkarte expires

Submitting the change-of-purpose request while your Chancenkarte is still valid is far smoother than waiting until close to its expiry. If there's a gap between your Chancenkarte ending and your course starting, ask the Ausländerbehörde about a temporary extension or fiction certificate (Fiktionsbescheinigung) to stay legally covered.

Different conversion paths

Your route depends on your qualification

SituationPath
You already meet university entrance requirementsApply directly to your chosen programme, then follow the Zweckwechsel steps above once admitted.
Your qualification needs a German entrance exam firstEnrol in a Studienkolleg (foundation course) or prepare for the Feststellungsprüfung while your Chancenkarte is active, then convert once you hold a full university admission letter.
Your passport is being renewed or reissuedYou'll generally need the new passport before the Ausländerbehörde can transfer and update your permit — start the renewal early so it doesn't stall your conversion.
You have close family with you on a dependent visaFamily reunion requirements are generally the same across Chancenkarte and student permits, but your family's visa timeline may need to be coordinated with yours — ask the Ausländerbehörde directly about sequencing.

Every case is reviewed individually

Outcomes depend on your specific Ausländerbehörde, your documents, and how much time is left on your Chancenkarte. Treat this page as a map of the process, not a guarantee — confirm your own situation with your local immigration office or a qualified immigration advisor before relying on any of these timelines.
