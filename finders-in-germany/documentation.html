04 — Documentation

The registration paperwork, in the right order

Germany's administrative steps unlock each other in sequence: your address registration produces the document your bank, tax office and university enrolment all depend on. Do them out of order and you'll be sent back to the start.

Within 14 days of moving in

Anmeldung — register your address

Every resident of Germany must register their address at the local Bürgeramt (citizens' office) within two weeks of moving in. Book an appointment as early as possible — slots in student cities fill up fast.

What to bring: passport, rental contract, and a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung (a landlord confirmation form your landlord must sign). What you get: a Meldebescheinigung (registration certificate) — keep several copies, you'll need it repeatedly.

Automatic, 2–4 weeks later

Tax ID (Steuer-Identifikationsnummer)

Once your Anmeldung is processed, the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern automatically posts your Tax ID to your registered address — you don't need to apply separately. This 11-digit number stays with you for life and is required by any employer before your first payslip.

If it doesn't arrive

If you haven't received your Tax ID after about four weeks, you can request it directly from the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern rather than waiting indefinitely, especially if a job offer is time-sensitive.

When you start your first paid job

Social Security Number (Sozialversicherungsnummer)

You don't need this before you arrive — it's generated when you take on your first paid job (including a Minijob). Your employer registers you with a Krankenkasse (statutory health insurer), which issues your Sozialversicherungsausweis. Bring your passport, Anmeldung certificate and Tax ID to your first day of work.

After Anmeldung

Open a German bank account

Most banks require your Meldebescheinigung and passport to open an account, and your account then receives the release from your blocked account. Many students use an online-first bank for lower fees and English-language support, alongside traditional branches like Sparkasse or Deutsche Bank.

Within 90 days of arrival (non-EU students)

Residence permit (Aufenthaltstitel)

Your entry visa is a temporary stamp; you'll need to convert it into a residence permit at the local Ausländerbehörde (foreigners' office) before it expires. Book this appointment as soon as you have your Anmeldung — combined with university enrolment and proof of funds, it's usually the longest wait on this list.

The order matters

Trying to open a bank account before Anmeldung, or applying for a residence permit before enrolling at your university, is the most common source of delay international students report. When in doubt, register your address first — nearly everything else depends on it.
